# 价格行为案例 03：2026-04-02 开盘反转与快速上行 | Price Action Case Study 03: Opening Reversal and Fast Rally on 2026-04-02

这是一篇关于 2026-04-02 的复盘案例研究。  
This is a case study of the 2026-04-02 session.

我认为当天开盘很典型，主要包括五个部分：开盘反转、快速上行、楔形失败、惊喜 K 线，以及之后的应对。  
The open was a useful example with five main themes: the opening reversal, the fast rally, the failed wedge, the surprise bar, and the follow-up response.

## 1. 开盘反转 | Opening Reversal

我倾向于重视电子盘盘前数据。  
I tend to give weight to the pre-market Globex session.

如果把盘前走势一起纳入分析，20:00 之后到开盘阴线附近的结构，可以界定为 5 分钟级别的 MTR 反转。  
Including the pre-market structure, the move from around 20:00 into the opening bear bar can be read as a 5-minute major trend reversal.

开盘阴线之后的反转，可以理解为第二入场点，也就是上涨的第二腿。  
The reversal after the opening bear bar can be treated as a second entry, or the second leg up.

TradingView chart: <https://www.tradingview.com/x/JrnR5uCs/>

这样看，结构会更清楚，做多信心也更充分。  
This reading makes the structure clearer and gives stronger confidence for the long side.

即使在上影线处挂突破单，也比较容易拿到 2R 的收益；之后面对 60 分钟 EMA 时，持仓信心也会更足。  
Even a stop entry above the upper tail could have reached 2R fairly easily, which would make it easier to hold when price approached the 60-minute EMA.

TradingView 图：<https://www.tradingview.com/x/UJ56RJOn/>  
TradingView chart: <https://www.tradingview.com/x/UJ56RJOn/>

如果把止损放到前面真实低点，那么第三根 K 线的 1R 目标也仍在均线下方。  
If the stop is placed below the true prior low, the 1R target from the third bar is still below the moving average.

所以无论如何，这个位置都有下单依据。  
Either way, this was a tradable location.

## 2. 更高周期证据 | Higher-Timeframe Evidence

如果把第四根 K 线高点作为入场点，只做 1R，目标正好接近第六根 K 线高点。  
If the fourth bar high is used as the entry and only 1R is targeted, the target lands near the sixth bar high.

当然，第四根 K 线本身已经在交易区间顶部，因此是否值得挂单仍然需要判断。  
Of course, the fourth bar was already near the top of the trading range, so the stop order still required judgment.

但如果把背景理解为 MTR，就可以给这笔交易更多期待。  
But if the background is read as an MTR, the trade deserves more room and expectation.

TradingView chart: <https://www.tradingview.com/x/nzrFXb9a/>

更多证据来自 1 小时周期：盘前低点正好接近 1 小时回调的 50% 位置。  
Additional evidence came from the 1-hour chart: the pre-market low was near the 50% pullback area.

这会增强对开盘后四根 K 线之后做多的信心。  
That increases confidence in taking the long after the first four bars.

## 3. 快速上行中的安全入场 | Safer Entry During the Fast Rally

快速上行阶段也有一个相对安全的进入点：1 分钟图上的 H2。  
During the fast rally, there was also a relatively safe entry: the H2 on the 1-minute chart.

TradingView 图：<https://www.tradingview.com/x/moyPG33q/>  
TradingView chart: <https://www.tradingview.com/x/moyPG33q/>

缩小到 1 分钟周期后，可以清楚看到这个 H2 入场点。  
On the 1-minute chart, the H2 entry was clear.

之后的 1 分钟 K 线都没有挑战 EMA20，因此这笔交易应该较容易拿到 1R，并有机会继续吃到后面的惊喜 K 线。  
The following 1-minute bars did not challenge the EMA20, so the trade should have reached 1R easily and offered a chance to hold for the later surprise bar.

TradingView chart: <https://www.tradingview.com/x/HSJHjzWe/>

事实上，市场也准确到达了 2R 位置。  
In fact, the market reached the 2R area precisely.

## 核心结论 | Key Takeaway

这一天的关键不是单独看某一根 K 线，而是把盘前结构、开盘反转、更高周期回调位置和 1 分钟 H2 入场结合起来。  
The key was not any single bar, but combining the pre-market structure, the opening reversal, the higher-timeframe pullback area, and the 1-minute H2 entry.

当这些证据叠加时，原本看似激进的突破单会变得更有逻辑。  
When those pieces align, what looks like an aggressive stop entry becomes much more logical.
