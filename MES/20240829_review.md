# MES / ES 复盘（2024-08-29） | MES / ES Review (2024-08-29)

## 核心观察 | Core Observation

盘前先形成了一个交易区间，开盘后跳空向上，并形成对盘前区间的突破。  
Before the open, price formed a trading range. The regular session then opened with an upside gap and broke above that pre-market range.

这一天的关键不是简单追多，而是判断开盘突破是否已经成功，还是仍需要二次测试与确认。  
The key question was not simply whether to buy the breakout, but whether the opening breakout had already succeeded or still needed a second test and confirmation.

## 开盘突破 | Opening Breakout

第 5 根 K 线对开盘跳空后的突破进行了测试，并且收盘后仍保留缺口。  
Bar 5 tested the breakout after the opening gap, and the gap remained open into the close of that bar.

如果要更稳妥地避免多头陷阱，可以不在第一次突破时追入，而是在第 4 根 K 线高点上方挂突破单。这样实际触发会落在第 6 根 K 线。  
To reduce the risk of buying a bull trap, a more conservative plan was to place a stop entry above the high of bar 4 rather than chasing the first breakout. That entry would be triggered on bar 6.

这个入场本身合理，但马上带来一个持仓问题：如果只按照 1 分钟开盘交易区间测算，第 7 根 K 线附近已经接近 1 倍 measured move 目标。  
That entry was reasonable, but it immediately created a trade-management question: if the target was measured only from the 1-minute opening range, bar 7 was already near a one-times measured-move objective.

因此 5645 附近出现了明显止盈，说明市场并没有把第 6 根 K 线的突破直接当成完全成功的突破处理。  
That is why clear profit-taking appeared near 5645, suggesting that the market did not yet treat the bar 6 breakout as a fully confirmed successful breakout.

## 两种持仓方案 | Two Trade-Management Plans

第一种方案是短线处理：第 7 根 K 线到达 1 分钟区间的 1 倍目标后先止盈离场，然后等待新的入场机会。  
The first plan was a short-term management approach: take profits once bar 7 reached the one-times target from the 1-minute range, then wait for a new entry.

第二种方案是按更大的结构持仓：结合前一日交易区间和开盘区间，只要价格没有跌回开盘区间下方，就继续把上方目标看向 5665 附近。  
The second plan was to manage the trade from the larger structure: by combining the prior day's range with the opening range, as long as price did not fall back below the opening range, the higher objective remained near 5665.

这两个方案都成立，但它们对应的是不同时间框架的交易逻辑。短线方案尊重 1 分钟区间的止盈反馈；波段方案尊重前一日区间与开盘区间重叠后的更大目标。  
Both plans were valid, but they came from different timeframes. The scalping plan respected the profit-taking response from the 1-minute range, while the swing plan respected the larger objective created by the prior-day range and the opening range.

## 二次确认 | Second Confirmation

第 11 根 K 线是对开盘区间突破的再次确认，也可以理解为对第 6 根 K 线突破的确认。  
Bar 11 was the second confirmation of the opening-range breakout. It also confirmed the breakout attempt from bar 6.

因此第 12 根 K 线可以作为新的入场点。此时目标有两个：一个是开盘交易区间测算出的 5654 附近，另一个是前一日交易区间与最近交易区间重叠后的 5665 附近。  
Therefore, bar 12 offered a new entry. At that point there were two objectives: the opening-range target near 5654, and the larger overlapping-range target near 5665.

第 15 根 K 线同样是合理入场点，因为它出现在二次确认之后，并且仍然没有破坏开盘突破结构。  
Bar 15 was also a reasonable entry because it appeared after the second confirmation and did not invalidate the opening breakout structure.

后续走势依次到达 5654 和 5665 两个重要盈利位置，验证了从开盘区间目标到更大区间目标的持仓路径。  
Price later reached both important profit objectives, 5654 and 5665, validating the management path from the opening-range target to the larger range-based target.

## 5665 磁力位后的多头风险 | Bull Risk After the 5665 Magnet

到达 5665 附近后，要特别注意第 31 根和第 32 根突破 K 线。它们可以作为多头入场信号，但也可以选择继续观察。  
After price reached the 5665 area, bars 31 and 32 required special attention. They could be treated as bull breakout entries, but waiting for more information was also reasonable.

原因是 5665 本身是一个非常大的磁力位置，也是多个交易区间共同指向的目标位。这个位置附近应该会有大量多头止盈。  
The reason is that 5665 was a major magnet and a shared objective from several trading ranges. Many bulls were likely to take profits around that area.

因此，对第 31 根和第 32 根突破入场的多头来说，他们很难再顺利达到自己的 1 倍 measured move 目标。  
Therefore, bulls who bought the bar 31 or bar 32 breakout were unlikely to easily reach their own one-times measured-move objective.

在这种背景下，如果后面产生反向的交易区间突破，就像第 43 根 K 线那样，这里就会成为一部分多头放弃的位置。  
In that context, if price later produced a breakout in the opposite direction from the trading range, as it did around bar 43, that area became a place where some bulls would give up.

如果价格进一步走到第 58 根 K 线附近，多头放弃会更多，所以那里是一个更好的空头入场位置。  
If price then moved further toward bar 58, more bulls would likely exit, making that area a better short entry location.

## 午后突破与失败回调 | Afternoon Breakout and Failed Pullback

后续多头在第 60 根 K 线之后再次突破，并顺利到达以第 60 根 K 线为基础测算的 1 倍盈亏比位置。  
Later, bulls broke out again after bar 60 and successfully reached the one-times reward-risk objective measured from the bar 60 area.

但之后的回调只回到前一段下跌的 50% 附近就停止前进，并重新向下运动。  
However, the following pullback stalled around the 50% retracement of the prior selloff and then turned down again.

这说明当天后半段并不是单纯的持续多头趋势，而是在大目标位完成后反复出现多头止盈、多头放弃和空头反击。  
This shows that the second half of the day was not a simple continuous bull trend. After the major target was reached, the market repeatedly showed bull profit-taking, bull exits, and bear counterattacks.

## 复盘结论 | Review Takeaways

- 第 6 根 K 线突破可以入场，但第 7 根 K 线的止盈反馈说明突破仍需要确认。  
- The breakout on bar 6 was tradable, but the profit-taking response on bar 7 showed that the breakout still needed confirmation.
- 如果按 1 分钟区间交易，5645 附近先止盈是合理选择。  
- If trading from the 1-minute range, taking profits near 5645 was reasonable.
- 如果按前一日交易区间和开盘区间管理，只要不跌破开盘区间，就可以继续看向 5665。  
- If managing the trade from the prior-day range and opening range, holding toward 5665 was reasonable as long as price stayed above the opening range.
- 第 11 根 K 线提供了关键确认，使第 12 根和第 15 根 K 线成为更清晰的顺势入场点。  
- Bar 11 provided the key confirmation, making bars 12 and 15 cleaner trend-following entries.
- 5665 是多个交易区间的共同目标，价格到达后要主动防范多头止盈和后续多头放弃。  
- 5665 was a shared target from multiple trading ranges, so after price reached it, bull profit-taking and later bull exits had to be anticipated.
- 第 43 根和第 58 根附近的反向突破，分别代表多头开始放弃和更多多头放弃，是后半天重要的空头观察点。  
- The reverse breakouts around bars 43 and 58 represented initial and broader bull exits, making them important short-side observation points in the second half of the day.
- 第 60 根之后的多头突破达成 1 倍目标，但后续回调只到下跌 50% 附近就失败，说明空头仍在控制后半天的节奏。  
- The bull breakout after bar 60 reached its one-times target, but the following pullback failed near the 50% retracement of the selloff, showing that bears still controlled the later session rhythm.
