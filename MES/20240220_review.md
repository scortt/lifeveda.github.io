# MES / ES 复盘（2024-02-20） | MES / ES Review (2024-02-20)

## 核心观察 | Core Observation

这一天的复盘重点，是不要只看单根强 K 线，而是把突破、跟随、回调和再次测试放在同一个结构里判断。

The focus of this review is not to judge the day from one strong bar alone, but to read the breakout, follow-through, pullback, and retest as one structure.

图表上的标注应作为主要锚点：先确认市场是否已经脱离前一个交易区间，再判断后续回调是在形成二次入场，还是在暴露突破失败。

The markings on the chart should be treated as the main anchors: first decide whether price has truly moved away from the prior trading range, then judge whether the pullback is setting up a second entry or exposing a failed breakout.

## 开盘后的结构判断 | Opening Structure

开盘后的第一步，不是急着给方向下结论，而是先判断价格是否仍在开盘区间内反复测试。

The first task after the open is not to rush into a directional conclusion, but to decide whether price is still testing inside the opening range.

如果突破离开区间后没有足够的连续跟随，交易者应该默认市场仍可能回到区间内部，而不是马上把它当作单边趋势日处理。

If a breakout away from the range does not get enough consecutive follow-through, traders should assume that price can still rotate back into the range instead of immediately treating the day as a one-sided trend day.

## 突破与跟随 | Breakout and Follow-Through

真正值得跟踪的突破，不只是突破点本身，而是突破后的下一段是否能让反方向交易者开始放弃。

The breakout worth tracking is not just the breakout point itself, but whether the next leg is strong enough to make traders on the other side start giving up.

如果突破后很快出现深回调，说明市场还没有完全接受新的价格区间。此时更好的处理方式，是等待回调后的再次突破或二次确认。

If a deep pullback appears soon after the breakout, the market has not fully accepted the new price area. In that case, the better approach is to wait for another breakout or second confirmation after the pullback.

## 回调确认 | Pullback Confirmation

回调的质量决定了后续入场的清晰度。浅回调并快速恢复，通常说明趋势方仍然有控制力；深回调并重新回到突破点附近，则说明突破还需要更多证据。

The quality of the pullback determines how clean the next entry is. A shallow pullback followed by quick recovery usually means the trend side still has control; a deep pullback back toward the breakout point means the breakout needs more evidence.

在标注图表上，可以把回调低点或高点当作后续交易管理的分界线。只要价格没有有效破坏这个分界线，原来的突破逻辑仍然可以继续跟踪。

On the marked chart, the pullback low or high can be used as a trade-management boundary. As long as price does not clearly break that boundary, the original breakout logic can still be tracked.

## 交易管理 | Trade Management

这类交易最容易犯的错误，是在第一段突破后过早假设趋势已经完成，而忽略后续二次确认。

The common mistake in this type of trade is assuming the trend is already complete after the first breakout leg, while ignoring the later second confirmation.

如果入场来自突破，止损和止盈应该围绕突破点、回调确认点和 measured move 目标来管理，而不是只根据单根 K 线的强弱临时决定。

If the entry comes from a breakout, the stop and target should be managed around the breakout point, the pullback confirmation point, and the measured-move objective, instead of being decided only from the strength of one bar.

## 复盘结论 | Review Takeaways

- 标注图表的核心价值，是把开盘区间、突破点、回调确认点和目标位放在同一张结构图里复查。
- The value of the marked chart is that it lets us review the opening range, breakout point, pullback confirmation, and target area in one structural view.
- 突破本身不是结论；突破后的跟随和回调质量，才决定这笔交易是否值得继续持有。
- The breakout itself is not the conclusion; the follow-through and pullback quality decide whether the trade deserves to be held.
- 如果回调没有破坏关键分界线，二次确认后的入场通常比第一次追突破更清晰。
- If the pullback does not break the key boundary, the entry after second confirmation is often cleaner than chasing the first breakout.
